### Job Description

**Organization Profile/Position Summary:**  
Accessibility NextGen is a mission-driven startup dedicated to streamlining and improving how  
individuals access resources for home accessibility. We leverage a modern, high-performance  
tech stack to bridge the gap between need and solutions. Under the supervision of our Chief  
Architect, the Full Stack Engineering Intern will assist in building out both the front-end and  
back-end of our platform. The intern can expect to participate in the development of new  
features, manage cloud deployments, and work with cutting-edge open-source identity and  
infrastructure tools.  
<br/>**What You Will Learn:**  
● Full Stack Development: How to build and maintain features using TypeScript for the  
frontend and Go for high-performance backend services.  
● Modern Identity Management: Hands-on experience with Zitadel for self-hosted  
authentication and user management.  
● Cloud Infrastructure & Deployment: Learning the "DevOps" side of things by  
deploying and managing applications on Fly.io.  
● System Architecture: How to navigate a real-world codebase and understand how  
self-hosted open-source tools integrate into a startup ecosystem.  
Additional Information: \* Process: Only a resume is required for the initial application.  
● Compliance: Selected candidates may be required to sign a standard Non-Disclosure  
Agreement (NDA) regarding proprietary workflows and company data

### Qualifications

Qualifications: ● Current college student—enrolled in the Computer Science or related program at CNM preferred. \* Exposure to or interest in TypeScript and Go (Golang). ● Familiarity with web fundamentals (HTML/CSS) and RESTful APIs. ● Organized student with a positive attitude and a desire to learn how to build scalable, production-ready software. ● Willingness to work with modern deployment and identity tools.
