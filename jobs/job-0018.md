### Job Description

**Information Technology Intern — Vault Studios ABQ**  
**Internship length: 135 hours**

The IT Intern will support the technology infrastructure used for film, audio, visual effects, and software production at Vault Studios. Working under designated technical staff, the intern will gain experience maintaining reliable workstations, troubleshooting technical issues, and supporting users in a professional studio environment.

**Responsibilities include:**

- Assist with workstation setup, approved software installation, updates, and peripheral configuration.
    
- Troubleshoot hardware, software, and network connectivity issues.
    
- Help maintain equipment inventories and technical documentation.
    
- Support shared storage, file access, and backup checks under supervision.
    
- Assist with approved account and access setup while protecting confidential project information.
    
- Document support requests, resolutions, and internship hours.
    

**Learning outcomes:** Build practical skills in technical support, system maintenance, networking fundamentals, data protection, and clear communication with technical and nontechnical users.

### Qualifications

You must be a credit-bearing CNM student enrolled in a program with an internship course to qualify for this internship. Not sure if your program has an internship course? Email internships@cnm.edu to find out. CNM Ingenuity learners are not eligible for this internship. You will be provided a stipend of \$765 per credit hour you register for, so for a 3 credit internship course, you will receive a \$2,295 stipend upon successful completion of the internship.
