Organization Profile / Position Summary

B&D Industries, Inc. is a family-owned and operated electrical, mechanical, plumbing, technology, and HVAC contractor with more than 65 years of experience delivering construction and service solutions. Offered in partnership with CNM, B&D's paid internship program provides students the opportunity to work alongside experienced professionals while gaining hands-on, real-world experience in a collaborative environment. The Developer Intern will support B&D's Business Applications and Data Integration team with entry-level development, system integration, workflow automation, documentation, testing, and data-quality tasks. The intern will work under IT leadership and experienced developers while learning how enterprise systems are connected, secured, supported, and improved.

Responsibilities

    Assist with developing and maintaining integrations between enterprise systems, including construction platforms, ERP/accounting systems, CRM tools, and Microsoft 365.
    Support basic workflow automations using approved Microsoft tools, including Power Platform and related technologies.
    Assist with API-based integration tasks using REST/JSON concepts under the direction of experienced developers.
    Help test integrations, automations, and system changes; document results and report defects or unexpected behavior.
    Assist with monitoring existing integrations for reliability, performance, and data accuracy.
    Help troubleshoot assigned integration or automation issues and escalate complex problems as directed.
    Create and maintain entry-level technical documentation, including workflow diagrams, data mappings, troubleshooting notes, and support procedures.
    Support data-quality activities by identifying duplicate records, missing information, synchronization issues, and inconsistent formats.
    Participate in version-control processes using Git-based platforms such as Azure DevOps or GitHub.
    Follow established development practices for code management, testing, peer review, logging, error handling, and deployment.
    Maintain traceability of work through Helpdesk tickets, change records, source control, and supporting documentation.
    Collaborate with senior and junior developers, IT staff, CIM, and business stakeholders to understand requirements and improve workflows.
    Follow least-privilege access, data-handling, change-management, and secure-development practices at all times.
    Participate in IT department training and learning opportunities to build foundational development, integration, and professional skills.
    Physical demands include long hours of sitting, prolonged computer use, standing, pulling/pushing, walking, lifting, and climbing.
    Other duties, activities, and responsibilities may vary and change as assigned.

What You Will Learn

    How enterprise construction, accounting, CRM, Microsoft 365, and business applications exchange data.
    Fundamentals of API-based integrations, workflow automation, and data mapping.
    How to test, document, troubleshoot, and support integrations in a professional IT environment.
    Foundational version-control practices, including commits, branches, pull requests, and code review.
    How secure development, access control, change management, and audit-ready documentation support business systems.
    Professional communication, requirements gathering, collaboration, time management, and problem-solving skills.
    How an IT development team supports operational efficiency across field and administrative teams.

*This role is contingent on passing a background check and pre-employment drug test.
Qualifications
Current CNM student (enrolled in Fall 2026 classes at CNM) in Computer Science, Computer Information Systems, Software Development, Information Technology, or related degree program preferred. High school diploma or equivalent required. Basic understanding of programming concepts, databases, APIs, data integration, or workflow automation preferred. Familiarity with Microsoft 365, Power Platform, Visual Studio, Azure DevOps, GitHub, or similar tools is helpful but not required. Basic analytical and problem-solving skills with attention to detail. Strong communicator who is organized, dependable, curious, and eager to learn. Ability to follow structured development, documentation, security, and governance processes.
