**Job Summary**

**Job Description**

We are looking for a **Cybersecurity Analyst Intern** to participate in a structured cybersecurity internship program focused on security operations, vulnerability management, threat analysis, incident response, system hardening, and governance, risk, and compliance activities within a simulated client environment. This position will be **Part-Time** and **Remote** .

Only work authorizations that will not require sponsorship now or in the future will be considered.

**What You'll Do**

- Perform asset inventory activities and establish a security baseline for a simulated client network
    
- Conduct vulnerability assessments and document findings, risks, and remediation recommendations
    
- Analyze attack vectors and cybersecurity threats impacting the simulated client environment
    
- Implement and document approved system hardening activities within a controlled environment
    
- Participate in incident response exercises and create detailed incident reports
    
- Perform security operations activities, including alert triage and monitoring system tuning
    
- Assist with governance, risk, and compliance initiatives, including drafting security policies and risk documentation
    
- Develop and present a capstone security program summary for client leadership
    
- Attend daily standups and complete weekly written deliverables
    
- Incorporate feedback from Elysium Cyber staff into ongoing assignments and deliverables
    
- Work within an isolated AWS-based training environment accessed through a web browser
    
- Collaborate with mentors and technical staff to achieve weekly learning objectives
    

**What You'll Need**

Required:

- Enrollment in or recent completion of a degree, certificate, or training program in Cybersecurity, Information Technology, Computer Science, or a related field
    
- Basic understanding of cybersecurity principles, networking concepts, operating systems, and security best practices
    
- Strong written communication skills with the ability to create professional reports and technical documentation
    
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
    
- Ability to work independently and manage tasks in a remote environment
    
- Reliable computer and internet connection
    
- Ability to complete weekly assignments and deliverables within established timelines
    

Preferred:

- Familiarity with AWS cloud concepts and services
    
- Exposure to vulnerability assessment tools, security monitoring platforms, or SIEM technologies
    
- Experience with cybersecurity labs, coursework, capture-the-flag competitions, or personal security projects
    
- Understanding of incident response processes and cybersecurity frameworks
    
- Knowledge of governance, risk, and compliance concepts
    
- Previous internship, academic project, or hands-on experience related to cybersecurity
