### Terra Vera

Changing the status quo in agriculture  
After facing significant harvest losses due to pests and diseases in controlled environment agriculture, we set out to find practical solutions. Unfortunately, we found that most available options were either too expensive, didn’t work, or posed safety risks.  
That’s when Terra Vera was born. We wanted to support farmers who are already struggling with tight margins while ensuring they can grow healthy crops without bringing harmful toxins back to their families. Our mission is to empower growers and beekeepers to produce food that is safe and free from harmful chemicals, ensuring the health of both their families and the wider community.
