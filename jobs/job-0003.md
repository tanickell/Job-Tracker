### Organization Profile/Position Summary:  
Accessibility NextGen is a mission-driven startup dedicated to streamlining and improving the  
way individuals access resources for home accessibility. We leverage technology to bridge the  
gap between need and solutions. Under the supervision of our Chief Architect, the AI  
Engineering Intern will play a critical role in enhancing our internal AI processes. The intern can  
expect to participate in the development, testing, and refinement of our Large Language Model  
(LLM) implementations and prompt engineering workflows to improve the accuracy and  
helpfulness of our platform.

### Qualifications:

Qualifications: ● Current college student—enrolled in the Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning program at CNM preferred. ● Foundational understanding of Prompt Engineering and LLM concepts. ● Basic experience with programming languages (Python or Go preferred). ● Strong problem-solving skills and a desire to experiment with emerging AI technologies. ● Organized student with a positive attitude and a willingness to learn in a fast-paced startup environment.
