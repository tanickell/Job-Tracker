### Job Description
**TS-Nano Lab Technician**

Location: Albuquerque, New Mexico Company: TS-Nano

TS-Nano produces nanomodified polymer sealants that solve the pressing environmental problem of methane leaking from oil and gas wells into the atmosphere. We are seeking a Lab Technician to join our Albuquerque manufacturing operation. This Lab Technician role is ideal for community college students or recent graduates seeking hands-on laboratory experience in advanced materials, manufacturing, and quality control.

If you’re curious, motivated, and eager to learn how science turns into real-world products that address important societal problems, this is a great opportunity to build your skills and grow your career.

**What You’ll Do**

As a TS-Nano Lab Technician, you’ll support both laboratory and manufacturing operations while learning industry-standard practices:

· Organize and maintain laboratory equipment and workspaces

· Follow safety procedures for chemical handling, labeling, storage, and disposal

· Assist with inventory tracking, materials handling, and organization of lab supplies

· Assist with laboratory testing of solids, liquids, and gases used in production and research

· Help set up and run experiments, tests, and measurements using lab instruments (training provided)

· Monitor product quality and help document results to ensure products meet specifications

· Safely move materials and products as needed (must be able to safely lift up to 50 lbs)

· Work closely with lab and manufacturing team members to solve problems and improve processes

· Learn and follow OSHA and company safety guidelines

**What We’re Looking For**

This role is designed for early-career candidates who are ready to learn:

· Currently enrolled in or recently graduated from a community college or technical program in a STEM field (chemistry, materials science, engineering, manufacturing, or related field preferred)

· Little to no prior lab experience required — on-the-job training will be provided

· Basic computer skills (Microsoft Word, Excel, email, or similar tools)

· Strong attention to detail and willingness to follow written procedures

· Good communication skills and ability to work well in a team environment

· Curious mindset with an interest in science, materials, and problem-solving

· Ability to perform hands-on lab and material-handling tasks, including lifting up to 50 lbs

**Nice-to-Have (But Not Required)**

· Coursework or interest in chemistry, polymers, materials science, or manufacturing

· Familiarity with lab safety practices or chemical handling

· Interest in reducing the environmental impact from the oil & gas industry

**Why Start Your Career at TS-Nano?**

· Hands-on experience with cutting-edge materials and manufacturing processes

· Supportive team environment with mentoring and training

· Great opportunity for students or recent graduates to gain industry experience

· Learn skills that translate into long-term careers in lab science, manufacturing, or materials engineering
