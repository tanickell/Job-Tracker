### Job Description

Title:

KBR Mission Technology Solutions (MTS) - Defense & Intel Internship Program

Who We Are

At KBR Mission Technology Solutions (MTS), we deliver innovative solutions that support some of the nation's most critical defense, intelligence, space, and federal missions. Our teams provide expertise across engineering, software development, cybersecurity, data analytics, mission operations, logistics , science, and program management, helping customers solve complex challenges and drive mission success. KBR's commitment to innovation, collaboration, and technical excellence enables us to support the rapidly evolving needs of our customers while creating opportunities for employees to make a meaningful impact.

The Opportunity

KBR is seeking highly motivated and talented students to join our Defense & Intel Internship Program. This internship offers hands-on experience, professional development, and the opportunity to contribute to meaningful projects that support national security and advanced technology initiatives.

Interns will work alongside experienced professionals on real-world assignments, gaining exposure to technical and operational challenges while building valuable skills and industry knowledge. Whether your interests are in engineering, software, cyber, data analytics, science, business, or mission operations, KBR provides an environment where you can learn, contribute, and grow.

What You'll Do

As a KBR Defense & Intel Intern, you may have opportunities to:

- Support engineering, technology, cybersecurity, intelligence, operations, logistics , science, business, or program management activities.
    
- Assist with research, analysis, design, development, testing, integration, or process improvement efforts.
    
- Contribute to projects that address real-world customer challenges and mission requirements.
    
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to develop innovative solutions and deliver project objectives.
    
- Analyze data, prepare reports, and communicate findings to project teams and leadership.
    
- Participate in technical reviews, team meetings, and professional development activities.
    
- Gain exposure to industry tools, technologies, and best practices relevant to your area of study.
    
- Present project outcomes and recommendations to stakeholders at the conclusion of the internship.
