**Software Engineering Intern - DevOps - Carmel, IN or Golden, CO**

**Software Engineering Intern - DevOps**

The Software Engineering Intern - DevOps supports Allegion's Global Software Solutions team by learning to help deploy, secure, and monitor our cloud-based applications and infrastructure. As a Software Engineering Intern, you will work alongside experienced DevOps engineers in a dynamic, Agile environment, gaining hands-on exposure to Azure, CI/CD pipelines, and automation tooling while being mentored throughout your internship.

*Qualified candidates must be legally authorized to be employed in the United States. The company does not intend to provide sponsorship for employment visa status (e.g., H-1B, TN, etc.) for this employment position.*

**What You Will Do:**

- Support development teams in deploying, monitoring, and troubleshooting application releases across our Azure environments
    
- Help build and maintain automation scripts and CI/CD pipelines using tools such as Azure DevOps, PowerShell, and Terraform
    
- Assist in monitoring uptime, performance, and other operational metrics, and help drive open issues to resolution
    
- Partner with IT, Information Security, and Product Engineering teams on assigned projects
    
- Participate in Agile/Scrum ceremonies and contribute to team DevOps initiatives
    
- Explore new cloud technologies alongside your mentor and share what you learn with the team
    

**What You Need to Succeed:**

- Currently pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field (Required)
    
- Coursework or project experience with a scripting or programming language such as PowerShell, Python, or Unix shell scripting (Required)
    
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills (Required)
    
- Self-motivated, curious, and comfortable working in a team environment (Required)
    
- Foundational understanding of cloud computing concepts, Microsoft Azure preferred (Preferred)
    
- Basic understanding of version control (e.g., Git) and CI/CD concepts (Preferred)
