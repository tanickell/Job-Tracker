### Job Description

**What You Will Do**

Join the Chief Information Office at Los Alamos National Laboratory as an undergraduate intern. Learn, grow, and support unclassified and classified IT operations, configuration management, and technical services while gaining hands-on experience in networking, telecommunications, software development, analytics, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, customer service, project management, and other technology fields.

Successful candidates will be paired with a mentor and assigned to a team based on their skills, interests, and the needs of the organization and may be placed with one of the following divisions:

- ADS - Application and Data Services
    
- CISO - Chief Information Security Office
    
- ICE - Innovative Customer Experience
    
- ISD - Infrastructure Services Division
    
- PSE - Program Strategy and Execution
    

For full consideration and eligibility, candidates must be pursuing one of the following degrees (or equivalent): Computer Science, Information Technology, Cybersecurity, Management Information Systems (MIS), Software Engineering, Computer Engineering or Network Administration /Engineering. All candidates must have and maintain a minimum of 3.0 GPA on a 4.0 scale for consideration and be enrolled in a minimum of 12 credit hours each semester. To apply, please submit a cover letter, resume and current transcript.

**What You Need**

**Minimum Job Requirements:**

- Desire to learn and build knowledge within the realm of Information and Technology
    
- Desire to work in a customer service environment
    
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills
    
- Effective time management skills, detail oriented, and organized
    
- Experience with collaborating effectively within team environments
    
- Knowledge of, or courses taken in Windows, Mac OS X desktop administration, or Linux
    
- Knowledge of, or courses taken in solving software and hardware issues
    

**Education/Experience:** Enroll in and successfully complete a minimum of 12 undergraduate credit hours per semester (or the equivalent of full-time enrollment) in a Computer Science, Information Technology, (or relevant IT focus) major. Maintain a cumulative GPA of 3.0 or above on a 4.0 scale.

**Desired Qualifications:**

- Experience in delivering exceptional customer service
    
- Knowledge of and /or experience administrating Linux, Windows, or Mac systems
    
- Knowledge of and/or experience with hardware or software applications
    
- Knowledge of and/or experience within realm of Cybersecurity
    
- PMP or related Scrum certifications (CSM, PSM, PSPO, or similar)
